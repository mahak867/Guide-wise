# Hands-on test with fellow students: 15 minutes each

Goal: real evidence for usability, closing the Round 1 commitment we missed. Run it with 3 to 5 people who have not seen Guidewise. Do not help them unless they are stuck for more than a minute (that counts as "needed help").

## Before each session

- Open https://guidewise-kohl.vercel.app in a fresh incognito window (each visitor gets a fresh workspace).
- Have a timer ready. Write down what they say out loud.

## Say this

"This tool checks a marketing post before it goes out. You are Riya, who writes posts for a coaching centre. Please think aloud while you work. We are testing the tool, not you."

## Tasks

| # | Task (read it out) | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Check the sample post and tell me the most serious problem it found. | They name a critical finding, such as the guaranteed-selection claim |
| 2 | Fix everything that can be fixed automatically. | Open findings drop after Accept all |
| 3 | The designer sent a corrected image. Swap it in. | "Images updated and checked again." |
| 4 | Send it for approval. Who has to approve it, and why? | They say compliance, because of an outcome claim |
| 5 | You are now Kavya from compliance. Approve it. | Approval receipt shown |
| 6 | Paste a real post from any coaching centre's Instagram and check it. | They read at least one finding and say whether it is right |
