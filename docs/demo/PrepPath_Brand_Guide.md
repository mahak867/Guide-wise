# PrepPath Brand Guide

How PrepPath Academy writes and designs its marketing. Each section is the source of one rule in the Guidewise demo Brand Kit; the rule ID is given after the heading.

## 1. No guaranteed outcomes

Rule R-12.

We never promise or hint at a guaranteed selection, rank, result or admission. Following the CCPA coaching guidelines (2024), phrases such as "100% selection" or "you will clear JEE" are not used anywhere.

## 2. Superlatives need evidence

Rule R-08.

Words such as "#1", "best" or "top" are used only when an approved fact in the Facts Register supports them. Without that fact, describe what we do instead.

## 3. Figures come from the Facts Register

Rule R-CLAIM.

Every number we publish must match an approved fact that is valid on the day of publication, be used for the subject that fact is about, and carry the disclosure the fact requires.

## 4. Naming successful students

Rule R-STU.

A successful student is named only together with the course they took with us, and only with their written consent dated after the result.

## 5. The results disclaimer

Rule R-DISC.

Any post that mentions selections, ranks or results carries disclaimer D-1, in the same language as the claim.

## 6. Contact details

Rule R-15.

Published content gives only the approved admissions contacts. A staff member's personal email or phone number is never published.

## 7. No false urgency

Rule R-10.

We do not pressure readers with scarcity or countdowns. If seats or time are limited, we state the real date or the real number of seats.

## 8. Confident, never hype

Rule T-2.

PrepPath sounds calm and sure of itself. We do not shout: no stacked exclamation marks and no words in capitals for emphasis.

## 9. Say "adaptive practice"

Rule TERM-4.

Our product term is "adaptive practice". We do not call it "AI-powered".

## 10. Say "affordable"

Rule TERM-2.

We describe our fees as affordable. "Cheap" undersells the programme.

## 11. Brand colours

Rule VIS-PAL.

Any colour that covers a meaningful part of a creative is one of our palette colours or a neutral: white, grey or black.

## 12. Alt text

Rule VIS-ALT.

Every image we publish carries alt text that describes it and repeats any words shown in it, as WCAG 2.2 asks (success criterion 1.1.1).

## 13. Contrast

Rule VIS-CON.

The main colours of a creative contrast by at least 4.5:1, so text placed on them stays readable (WCAG 2.2, success criterion 1.4.3).

## 14. The PrepPath mark

Rule VIS-LOGO.

Every creative carries the approved PrepPath mark, unaltered in colour and proportion, at least 8% of the image width, with clear space of a quarter of its height on every side.

## 15. Text size in creatives

Rule VIS-TEXT.

Text in a creative is at least 16 pixels high on a 1080-pixel-wide post, scaled to the image's width, so it can be read on a phone.

## 16. One campaign, one set of facts

Rule CONS-1.

A price, figure, date or promo code is the same in the caption, in every creative and in every campaign document. Two different prices for one offer can mislead a buyer.

## 17. No hidden characters

Rule HID-1.

Our text contains no zero-width spaces, direction overrides or soft hyphens. Such characters can hide a phrase from review.

## 18. People in creatives

Rule VIS-PEOPLE.

A creative that shows a person, above all a student, is published only when that person's written consent is on record.

## 19. Writing our name

Rule NAME-1.

PrepPath is written as one word with two capital Ps. Web and email addresses keep their lower case.

## 20. No capitals for emphasis

Rule STY-CAPS.

We do not set words in capitals to shout. Exam names and short acronyms such as JEE, NEET, AIR and IIT are fine.

## 21. Inclusive language

Rule INC-1.

We describe students by what they need, not by labels, and we address every student, not only boys.

## 22. Ad text length

Rule STY-AD.

The offer and any required disclaimer come first in ad text: anything after 125 characters is hidden behind "See more".

## 23. Short sentences

Rule STY-READ.

Keep sentences to 30 words or fewer. Most of our readers see our posts on a phone.

## 24. Full links on social posts

Rule LINK-1.

Social posts and ads show the full preppath.in address. We do not use shortened links, because they hide where they lead.

## 25. Speak to the student

Rule T-5.

We address the reader directly as "you" and avoid talking about students in the third person.

## 26. Paid partnerships

Rule AD-PAID.

A post made with or paid for by PrepPath carries #ad, #sponsored or "Paid partnership" where readers see it, as the ASCI influencer guidelines require.

## 27. Offers and their end dates

Rule OFFER-1.

A discount, a percentage off, something free or a promo code always states when the offer ends, or carries "T&C apply", in the same text.

## 28. Competitors

Rule COMP-1.

We do not name another coaching institute. Any comparison with a competitor is the brand manager's decision and needs evidence on file.

## 29. Link text

Rule LINK-2.

Link text says where the link leads. "Click here", "tap here" or "read more" on its own is not used; "Link in bio" is fine.

## 30. Hashtags

Rule TAG-1.

Each word in a hashtag starts with a capital, as in #JeePreparation, so screen readers read the words. Single-word hashtags are fine.

## 31. Punctuation and emoji

Rule STY-EMOJI.

Use one exclamation or question mark at a time, and no more than 3 emoji in a social post.
